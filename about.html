How we started

A side project that outgrew the spreadsheet

Solstice Travel Co. started in 2012 as one traveler's shared spreadsheet of hotels and guides she trusted. Friends kept asking her to just book it for them — so she did, then hired the first guide she'd ever used, then a second. Today it's a small team split between Chattogram and a rotating handful of countries, still working from the same principle: we only recommend what we'd book for our own families.

We're not a marketplace and we don't push package inventory we haven't personally checked. Every hotel, driver and guide on our list has been used by someone on our team, in the last two years.

What we hold ourselves to

Three things we won't compromise on

We've actually been there

Nothing goes on an itinerary until someone on our team has stayed, eaten, or hiked it themselves.

A person, not a ticket queue

You get one planner for the whole trip — the same person from first call to the day you land back home.

Straight pricing

What we quote is what you pay. No surprise resort fees or "processing charges" added later.

Help while you're actually traveling

A local contact reachable for the length of your trip, not just an emergency hotline nobody answers.

Milestones

Fourteen years, in short

2012

2015

First full-time guide hired; began offering fully-booked itineraries across Southeast Asia.

2018

Opened our Chattogram office and expanded into Europe and Japan routes.

2021

Added in-house visa processing support after one too many clients missed a trip over paperwork.

2026

Crossed 2,300 journeys planned, still run by a team small enough to know every trip by name.
